## What it is
BM25 is the standard scoring function for keyword search. It ranks documents by how well they match the query
words, with two corrections: a word counts for less the more often it repeats, and long documents are not
rewarded just for being long.

## How it works
For each query word, BM25 multiplies the word's rarity (inverse document frequency) by a saturating term frequency
and a length normalisation factor, then sums over the query words. Rare, specific words such as "RRF" dominate;
common words such as "the" contribute almost nothing.

## Pitfalls
BM25 knows nothing about meaning. "car" and "automobile" are unrelated to it. Its parameters (k1, b) are usually
left at defaults; tune them only with an evaluation set.
